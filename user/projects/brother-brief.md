# LifeOS — A Note to My Brother

Hey,

I want to run something by you. I've been building a side project and I think it could be something real — but I need a thought partner, and honestly, I want it to be you.

**The idea:** a personal AI OS. Not another chatbot, not a task app. Think of it as an AI layer that sits across your entire life — your calendar, email, goals, projects, habits — and starts autocompleting it. You tell it what you're working toward, and it starts doing the work. Scheduling, summarizing, planning, nudging. The goal is to get to a place where the gap between "I should do X" and "X is done" collapses.

**Why now:** The pieces just became real. Claude and GPT are actually good at reasoning and multi-step tasks. MCP (model-context protocol) means AI can plug into real tools natively. We're at the moment where this isn't a research project anymore — it's a product waiting to be assembled.

**Why us:** You know GenAI from the inside — how these models actually work, where they're strong, where they break. I've been shipping production software at scale for years at LinkedIn. I know how to build systems that don't fall apart. Together, we have both sides: the ML intuition and the engineering discipline. Most teams trying to build this have one or the other.

**What I've already built:** A working prototype. Right now, it has a chat interface, a knowledge base about me (my projects, routines, goals), web search, and a persistent memory loop. I'm using it today. It's not polished, but the bones are good — and it's shown me this idea has real legs.

**What I'm asking:** Start as a side project. A few hours a week. Help me think through the product direction, pick up pieces of the build, pressure-test the ideas. If it gets traction, we talk about going further. No pressure, no commitment beyond showing up curious.

I think we could build something genuinely useful — first for ourselves, then for a lot of other people who feel like life admin is eating them alive.

Worth a call?

— Shrey
